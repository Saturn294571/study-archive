5. `___` Under accrual accounting, transactions are recorded in the period in which the cash is received or paid.->F
6. `___` Supplies are usually recorded as an asset first, and the used portion is recognized as Supplies Expense later.->T
7. `___` Asset accounts normally have debit balances.->T

10. `___` Retained earnings is a permanent account.->t
