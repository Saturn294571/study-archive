### AutoCorrelation (자기상관)
* **개념:** $\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \dots, \epsilon_n$ 시차에 따른 상관성 존재
* **K 시차 자기상관:** $\text{Corr}(\epsilon_t, \epsilon_{t-k})$
    * $K=0 \implies \text{Corr}(\epsilon_t, \epsilon_t) = 1$
    * $K=1 \implies \text{Corr}(\epsilon_t, \epsilon_{t-1})$
    * $K \implies \text{Corr}(\epsilon_t, \epsilon_{t-k})$ ($\text{lag } K - \text{auto corr.}$)

#### 정상성 (Stationarity)
* **정의:**
    * 관측 확률 변수가 시간에 따라 결합 분포가 달라지지 않는다 (강 정상성)
    * or 관측 확률 변수의 1차 적률과 2차 적률이 시간에 따라 달라지지 않는다 (약 정상성)
        * $(E(X_t), E(X_t^2))$
#### 자기상관의 추정 (AutoCorrelation Function)
* **$K$-시차 자기상관:**
    * $(\epsilon_1, \epsilon_{k+1}), (\epsilon_2, \epsilon_{k+2}), \dots, (\epsilon_{n-k}, \epsilon_n) \implies \text{Corr}$
* **ACF ($K$):**
    * $\text{ACF}(K) = \text{Corr}(\epsilon_t, \epsilon_{t-k}) \text{ or } \text{Corr}(\epsilon_t, \epsilon_{t+k})$
    * $\rho(K) \implies \rho(0) = 1, \quad \rho(1) = \text{Corr}(\epsilon_t, \epsilon_{t-1})$

### Autoregressive model (order 1)
* **Model Definition**
    * $\epsilon_t = \rho \epsilon_{t-1} + w_t, \quad -1 < \rho < 1, \quad w_t \sim N(0, \sigma^2) \text{ indep}$
#### (정상성 조건)
* 1. **Expectation (기댓값)**
    * $E(\epsilon_t) = \rho E(\epsilon_{t-1}) + E(w_t) = \rho E(\epsilon_{t-1})$
    * $E(\epsilon_t) = E(\epsilon_{t-1}) = \eta \implies \eta = \rho(\eta) \implies (1 - \rho)\eta = 0, \quad \eta = 0$
* 2. **Variance (분산)**
    * $Var(\epsilon_t) = Var(\rho \epsilon_{t-1} + w_t), \quad \epsilon_{t-1} = \rho \epsilon_{t-2} + w_{t-1}$
    * $= Var(w_t) + Var(\rho (\rho \epsilon_{t-2} + w_{t-1}))$
    * $= Var(w_t) + \rho^2 Var(w_{t-1}) + Var(\rho^2 \epsilon_{t-2})$
    * **만약** $\epsilon_t, \epsilon_{t-1}, \epsilon_{t-2}, \dots$ (무한 등비급수 형태)
    * $\implies Var(\epsilon_t) = Var(\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \rho^k w_{t-k}) = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \rho^{2k} Var(w_{t-k})$
    * $= \sigma^2 \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \rho^{2k} = \sigma^2 \cdot \frac{1}{1 - \rho^2} \quad (-1 < \rho < 1)$
#### \* 추가 (Non-zero Mean Case)
* **만약** $\epsilon_t = \mu + \rho \epsilon_{t-1} + w_t$
* $E(\epsilon_t) = \mu + \rho E(\epsilon_{t-1}) + 0, \quad E(\epsilon_t) = E(\epsilon_{t-1}) = \eta$
* $(1 - \rho)\eta = \mu \implies \eta = \frac{\mu}{1 - \rho}$

### AR(1) Process
* **정의:** $\epsilon_t = \rho \epsilon_{t-1} + w_t, \quad w_t \sim iid N(0, \sigma^2)$
* **전개 (Expansion):**
    * $\epsilon_t = \rho \epsilon_{t-1} + w_t$
      $= w_t + \rho(\rho \epsilon_{t-2} + w_{t-1})$
      $= w_t + \rho w_{t-1} + \rho^2 \epsilon_{t-2}$
      $= w_t + \rho w_{t-1} + \rho^2(\rho \epsilon_{t-3} + w_{t-2})$
      $= w_t + \rho w_{t-1} + \rho^2 w_{t-2} + \rho^3 \epsilon_{t-3}$
      $= \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \rho^k w_{t-k}$
* **기댓값 및 분산:**
    * $E(\epsilon_t) = 0$
    * $Var(\epsilon_t) = \frac{\sigma^2}{1-\rho^2} \quad (-1 < \rho < 1)$
#### AutoCovariance (자기 공분산)
* **$k$ 시차 자기 공분산:**
    * $Cov(\epsilon_t, \epsilon_{t-k}) = Cov(\sum_{j=0}^{k-1} \rho^j w_{t-j} + \rho^k \epsilon_{t-k}, \epsilon_{t-k})$
	    * (참고: $w_t, w_{t-1}, \dots, w_{t-k-1}$ 과 $\epsilon_{t-k}$ 는 독립)
    * $\Rightarrow Cov(\epsilon_t, \epsilon_{t-k}) = Cov(\rho^k \epsilon_{t-k}, \epsilon_{t-k}) = \rho^k Cov(\epsilon_{t-k}, \epsilon_{t-k})$ $= \rho^k \frac{\sigma^2}{1-\rho^2}$
#### k 시차 자기상관 (AR(1))
* **Correlation:**
    * $Corr(\epsilon_t, \epsilon_{t-k}) = \frac{Cov(\epsilon_t, \epsilon_{t-k})}{\sqrt{Var(\epsilon_t)}\sqrt{Var(\epsilon_{t-k})}}$ $= \frac{\rho^k \frac{\sigma^2}{1-\rho^2}}{\frac{\sigma^2}{1-\rho^2}} = \rho^k$
### AR(1) Model with Mean ($w_i$)
* **모델 정의:**
    * $z_i = \rho z_{i-1} + \eta_i, \quad \eta_i \sim iid N(0, \sigma^2)$
    * $w_i = \mu + z_i, \quad E(z_i) = 0$
* **기초 통계량:**
    1. **기댓값:** $E(w_i) = E(\mu + z_i) = \mu$
    2. **분산:** $Var(w_i) = Var(z_i) = \frac{\sigma^2}{1-\rho^2}$
    3. **공분산 구조:**
		- $w_i = \mu + \eta_i + \rho z_{i-1}$;    $w_{i-1} = \mu + z_{i-1}$
		- $Cov(w_i, w_{i-1}) = Cov(\mu + \eta_i + \rho z_{i-1}, \mu + z_{i-1})$
			- $= Cov(\rho z_{i-1}, z_{i-1}) = \rho Var(z_{i-1})$
		* $Cov(w_i, w_{i-2}) = Cov(\mu + \eta_i + \rho \eta_{i-1} + \rho^2 z_{i-2}, \mu + z_{i-2})$
			* $= \rho^2 Cov(z_{i-2}, z_{i-2}) = \rho^2 Var(z_{i-2})$
#### 표본평균의 분산 ($Var(\bar{w})$)
* **전개 과정:**
    * $Var(\bar{w}) = Var(\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n w_i)$
    * $= \frac{1}{n^2} Var(\sum_{i=1}^n w_i) = \frac{1}{n^2} (\sum_{i=1}^n Var(w_i) + 2 \sum_{i<j} Cov(w_i, w_j))$
    * $= \frac{1}{n^2} \cdot n \cdot Var(w_i) + \frac{2}{n^2} \sum_{k=1}^{n-1} (n-k) \underbrace{Cov(w_i, w_{i-k})}_{(*)}$
	    * (\*) $\begin{pmatrix}(w_1, w_2) & (w_1, w_3) & \cdots & (w_1, w_n) \\& (w_2, w_3) & \cdots & (w_2, w_n) \\& & \ddots & \vdots \\& & & (w_{n-1}, w_n)\end{pmatrix}$
		    * **1행:** 총 $n-1$개의 항으로 구성됨.
		    * **시차 1:** $(w_1, w_2)$부터 $(w_{n-1}, w_n)$까지 대각선 방향 흐름은 시차가 1인 항들을 나타내며, 총 **$n-1$개**가 존재함.
* **결과 대입 ($Cov(w_i, w_{i-k}) = \rho^k Var(z_1)$):**
    * $= \frac{1}{n} Var(z_1) + \frac{2}{n^2} \sum_{k=1}^{n-1} (n-k) \rho^k Var(z_1)$

### Durbin-Watson Statistic

* **통계량 정의 ($d$):**
    $$ d = \frac{\sum_{t=2}^n (e_t - e_{t-1})^2}{\sum_{t=1}^n e_t^2} = \frac{\sum_{t=2}^n e_t^2 + \sum_{t=2}^n e_{t-1}^2 - 2\sum_{t=2}^n e_t e_{t-1}}{\sum_{t=1}^n e_t^2} $$

#### 근사식 유도 (Approximation)
* **가정 (Assumption):** 표본 크기 $n$이 충분히 클 때,
    * $\sum_{t=1}^n e_t^2 \approx \sum_{t=2}^n e_t^2 \approx \sum_{t=2}^n e_{t-1}^2$
    * (설명: $e_1^2 + \dots + e_n^2 \approx e_2^2 + \dots + e_n^2 \approx e_1^2 + \dots + e_{n-1}^2$)
* **전개:**
    * $d \approx 1 + 1 - 2 \frac{\sum_{t=2}^n e_t e_{t-1}}{\sum_{t=1}^n e_t^2} = 2 - 2\hat{\rho} = 2(1 - \hat{\rho})$
* **성질:**
    * $\hat{\rho} \approx 1 \Rightarrow d \approx 0$
    * $\hat{\rho} \approx 0 \Rightarrow d \approx 2$

#### 검정 규칙 (Decision Rule)
* **우측 검정 ($H_0: \rho = 0, \quad H_1: \rho > 0$):**
    * If $d < d_L$: **Reject $H_0$**
    * If $d > d_U$: **Do not reject $H_0$**
    * If $d_L < d < d_U$: **Inconclusive** (판정 보류)
* **좌측 검정 ($H_0: \rho = 0, \quad H_1: \rho < 0$):**
    * 통계량으로 $u = 4-d$를 사용하여 위와 동일한 기준 적용.
    * $4-d \approx 4 - (2 - 2\hat{\rho}) = 2(1+\hat{\rho})$
    * **참고:**
        * $\hat{\rho} \approx -1 \Rightarrow d \approx 4 \Rightarrow 4-d \approx 0$
        * $\hat{\rho} \approx 0 \Rightarrow d \approx 2 \Rightarrow 4-d \approx 2$
### Regression with Autocorrelated Errors (AR(1))

* **가정 ( Assumption):**
    * $\epsilon_t = \rho \epsilon_{t-1} + w_t, \quad w_t \sim iid N(0, \sigma^2)$ 관계가 있다면.
* **회귀 모형의 변형 과정:**
    1.  **기본 모형:**
        * $y_t = \beta_0 + \beta_1 x_t + \epsilon_t \quad \Rightarrow \quad \epsilon_t = y_t - \beta_0 - \beta_1 x_t$
        * $y_{t-1} = \beta_0 + \beta_1 x_{t-1} + \epsilon_{t-1} \quad \Rightarrow \quad \epsilon_{t-1} = y_{t-1} - \beta_0 - \beta_1 x_{t-1}$
    2.  **대입 (Substitution):**
        * $\Rightarrow y_t - \beta_0 - \beta_1 x_t = \rho(y_{t-1} - \beta_0 - \beta_1 x_{t-1}) + w_t$
    3.  **정리 (Rearrangement):**
        * $y_t - \rho y_{t-1} = \beta_0(1-\rho) + \beta_1(x_t - \rho x_{t-1}) + w_t$
* **변환된 모형 (Transformed Model):**
    * $$y_t^* = \beta_0^* + \beta_1 x_t^* + w_t$$
    * **변수 정의:**
        * $y_t^* = y_t - \rho y_{t-1}$
        * $x_t^* = x_t - \rho x_{t-1}$
        * $\beta_0^* = (1-\rho)\beta_0$
* **데이터 구조 변화:**
    * 원래 데이터: $(y_1, x_1), \dots, (y_n, x_n)$ ($n$개)
    * $\downarrow$
    * 변환된 데이터: $(y_2^*, x_2^*), \dots, (y_n^*, x_n^*)$ ($n-1$개)
* **$\rho$의 추정 (Unknown $\rho$):**
    * $\rho$를 모르므로 잔차 $(e_1, e_2, \dots, e_n)$를 이용하여 $\hat{\rho}$ 추정.
    * **절차:** 모형 진단(Residuals) $\rightarrow$ $\hat{\rho}$ 추정 $\rightarrow$ 결과 적용 (Transformation).
### 2-stage regression
1) 선형모형에 대해 OLS 적합 수행
2) $\rho$를 추정하여 $\hat{\rho}$를 계산
3) 추정된 $\hat{\rho}$를 통해 OLS 재적합 수행
#### 동시에 변수들을 최적화할 경우
* **목표:** $\rho, \beta_0, \beta_1$ 동시 최적화
* **목적 함수 (Objective Function):**
  $S(\rho, \beta_0, \beta_1) = \sum_{t=2}^n \left( y_t - \rho y_{t-1} - (1-\rho)\beta_0 - (x_t - \rho x_{t-1})\beta_1 \right)^2$
* **최적화 문제:**
  $(\hat{\rho}, \hat{\beta}_0, \hat{\beta}_1) = \underset{\rho, \beta_0, \beta_1}{\text{argmin}} \ S(\rho, \beta_0, \beta_1)$
    * **특성:** 이 함수는 전체 파라미터에 대해 **Non-convex** 형태임.
* **해결 전략 (Grid Search Approach):**
    1.  **$\rho$ 고정 (Fix $\rho$):**
        *   주어진 $\rho$에 대해 $\hat{\beta}_0, \hat{\beta}_1$를 찾는 문제는 **Convex**함 (선형 회귀).
    2.  **탐색 및 결정:**
        *   여러 $\rho$ 값을 탐색 (Grid Search) $\rightarrow$ $S$를 최소화하는 최적값 결정.
### Generalized Least Squares (GLS) with Spectral Decomposition

* **Model Assumptions:** 
  $Y = X\beta + \epsilon, \quad \epsilon \sim N(0, \Sigma_{n \times n})$
    * $\Sigma$ is a Covariance Matrix $\Rightarrow$ Positive Definite (양정치 행렬).
#### Spectral Decomposition
* **Decomposition:** $\Sigma = U D U'$
    * $U$: $\Sigma$의 고유벡터로 구성된 직교 행렬 ($UU' = U'U = I_n$).
    * $D$: $\Sigma$의 고유값을 대각성분으로 갖는 대각행렬 ($D = \text{diag}(d_{11}, \dots, d_{nn})$).

* **Matrix Operations:**
    * $\Sigma^{1/2} = U D^{1/2} U'$, where $\Sigma^{1/2}\Sigma^{1/2} = \Sigma$
    * $\Sigma^{-1/2} = U D^{-1/2} U'$
    * $D^{1/2} = \text{diag}(\sqrt{d_{11}}, \dots, \sqrt{d_{nn}})$
    * $D^{-1/2} = \text{diag}(\frac{1}{\sqrt{d_{11}}}, \dots, \frac{1}{\sqrt{d_{nn}}})$
#### Transformation & Estimation
* **Transformation Process:**
    * $Y = X\beta + \epsilon \Rightarrow \Sigma^{-1/2}Y = \Sigma^{-1/2}X\beta + \Sigma^{-1/2}\epsilon$
    * $\Rightarrow \tilde{Y} = \tilde{X}\beta + \tilde{\epsilon}$

* **Transformed Error:** 
  $\tilde{\epsilon} \sim N(0, I)$
* **GLS Estimator ($\hat{\beta}$):**
    * $\hat{\beta} = (\tilde{X}'\tilde{X})^{-1}\tilde{X}'\tilde{Y} = (X'\Sigma^{-1}X)^{-1}X'\Sigma^{-1}Y$

* **Variance ($Var(\hat{\beta})$):**
    * $Var(\hat{\beta}) = (X'\Sigma^{-1}X)^{-1} X'\Sigma^{-1} Var(Y) \Sigma^{-1} X (X'\Sigma^{-1}X)^{-1}$
    * $= (X'\Sigma^{-1}X)^{-1}$
