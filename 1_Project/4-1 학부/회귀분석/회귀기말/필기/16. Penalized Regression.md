### Variance Inflation Factor (VIF)

* **Model Assumptions:**
    * $Y_i = \beta_0 + \sum_{j=1}^p x_{ij}\beta_j + \epsilon_i$
* **Definition ($VIF_j$):**
    * $$VIF_j = \frac{1}{1-R_j^2}$$
    * $R_j^2$: Coefficient of determination ($R^2$) for the regression model $X_j = \beta_0 + \sum_{k \neq j} x_k \beta_k + \epsilon$.
* **Interpretation:**
    * If $R_j^2 \approx 1 \Rightarrow VIF_j \approx \infty$ (Perfect correlation).
    * If $R_j^2 \approx 0 \Rightarrow VIF_j \approx 1$ (No correlation).
    * **Rule of Thumb:** If $VIF_j > 10 \rightarrow$ Severe Multicollinearity.

#### Derivation Example (2 Variables)

* **Model:** $Y_i = \beta_1 x_{i1} + \beta_2 x_{i2} + \epsilon_i, \quad \epsilon_i \sim iid \ N(0, \sigma^2)$
    * Note: $Y, X_1, X_2$ are centered ($\sum y_i = 0, \sum x_{i1} = 0, \sum x_{i2} = 0$).
* **Auxiliary Regression ($X_2$ on $X_1$):**
    * $x_{i2} = \alpha x_{i1} + \eta_i$
    * $SSR_2 = \hat{\alpha}^2 \sum x_{i1}^2, \quad SST = \sum x_{i2}^2$
    * $R_2^2 = \frac{\hat{\alpha}^2 \sum x_{i1}^2}{\sum x_{i2}^2} = \frac{(\frac{\sum x_{i1}x_{i2}}{\sum x_{i1}^2})^2 \sum x_{i1}^2}{\sum x_{i2}^2} = \frac{(\sum x_{i1}x_{i2})^2}{(\sum x_{i1}^2)(\sum x_{i2}^2)}$
* **Variance of $\hat{\beta}$:**
    * $$Var(\hat{\beta}_1) = \sigma^2 \frac{\sum x_{i2}^2}{(\sum x_{i1}^2)(\sum x_{i2}^2) - (\sum x_{i1}x_{i2})^2}$$
    * **Simplification:**
        * Divide numerator and denominator by $(\sum x_{i1}^2)(\sum x_{i2}^2)$:
        * $$= \sigma^2 \frac{1}{\sum x_{i1}^2} \cdot \frac{1}{1 - \frac{(\sum x_{i1}x_{i2})^2}{(\sum x_{i1}^2)(\sum x_{i2}^2)}} = \frac{\sigma^2}{\sum x_{i1}^2} \cdot \frac{1}{1-R_2^2}$$
        * $$= \frac{\sigma^2}{S_{11}} \times VIF_1$$


### Condition Number ($k$)

* **Definition:** A measure of the overall multicollinearity.
* **Calculation:**
    * $\lambda_j$: $j$-th largest eigenvalue of $Corr(X)$ (상관행렬).
    * Order: $\lambda_1 \ge \lambda_2 \ge \dots \ge \lambda_p$
    * $$k = \sqrt{\frac{\lambda_1}{\lambda_p}} = \sqrt{\frac{\lambda_{max}}{\lambda_{min}}}$$
* **Thresholds (판단 기준):**
    * If $k > 15 \rightarrow$ Multicollinearity exists.
    * If $k > 30 \rightarrow$ Corrective action should always be taken.
